## Full description (target ≤4000 chars)

**LOG SETS. EARN POWER. EVOLVE.**

SaiyanStrong is a powerlifting and bodybuilding tracker built for lifters who want their training
log to feel like it's tracking something bigger than just numbers on a bar.

Every set you log adds to your **Power Level**. Heavier weight, more reps, bigger sets — it all
feeds your score. Cross a threshold and your **Saiyan stage evolves**, from Base Saiyan all the
way up to Ultra Instinct. It's the same training log you'd use anyway — just with a progression
system that makes consistency actually feel like it's building toward something.

**100% metric. Always.** No unit toggle to fight with, no lb/kg confusion — every weight, every
volume total, every 1RM estimate is in kilograms, everywhere in the app.

**Offline-first.** Log a workout in a basement gym with zero signal. SaiyanStrong works completely
offline — no account, no sign-in, no internet connection required for any core feature.

---

### WHAT'S INSIDE

**⚡ Fast set logging** — A flat, no-nonsense set table: SET / PREV / KG / REPS. Inline steppers
for quick weight and rep adjustments, one-tap set completion, and confirm-before-delete on every
destructive action so you never lose a workout by accident.

**⚡ Power Level & Saiyan evolution** — Your lifetime training volume drives a live Power Level
score and a scouter-style gauge on your home screen. A quick info sheet always shows you exactly
where you sit on the stage ladder and what's next.

**⚡ Rest timers that adapt to you** — Set a default rest timer, or override it per exercise —
squats get 3 minutes, curls get 60 seconds, and the app remembers.

**⚡ Templates & repeat-last-workout** — Save any session as a reusable template, or jump straight
back into your last workout with one tap from the Workout tab.

**⚡ 150+ exercise library** — Searchable, filterable by movement category, with equipment
variants (barbell vs. dumbbell vs. cable) tracked as distinct exercises so your history stays
accurate. Exercise detail pages include instructions, photos, personal records, and 1RM/volume
charts over time.

**⚡ Bodyweight & DOTS tracking** — Log bodyweight, watch a trend sparkline build, and get a live
DOTS score computed from your best squat/bench/deadlift — with a formula toggle for male/female
coefficients.

**⚡ History that tells a story** — Every session gets a Strong-style history card: date, sets,
best lift, PR badges, and total volume, grouped by month.

**⚡ Share your sessions** — Generate a shareable session card after every workout — total volume,
power earned, your current Saiyan stage — and send it straight to WhatsApp or wherever your
training partners are.

**⚡ Optional cloud backup** — Sign in with Google if you want your training history backed up
automatically after every workout. Skip it entirely and the app works exactly the same, fully
offline, forever. Your call.

### NO ADS. NO TRACKING. NO NOISE.

SaiyanStrong doesn't run ads, doesn't bundle analytics or tracking SDKs, and doesn't sell your
data. It's a training log first, and everything in it is built to get you back to your next set
faster — not to hold your attention hostage.

### WHO IT'S FOR

Powerlifters running a squat/bench/deadlift program. Bodybuilders tracking volume across a split.
Anyone who wants a training log with an actual sense of progression built in, not just a bare
spreadsheet with extra steps.

Log your first set. Watch your Power Level climb. See how far the evolution ladder goes.
